Launcher creates campaigns and ads from your workspace's creative library. Use it to prepare batches, reuse campaign settings, schedule work, and inspect provider results.

## The building blocks

| Object | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Media | Uploaded or imported image or video |
| Creative | A named family of content with saved versions |
| Approved creative version | Exact content eligible for selection |
| Collection | A fixed selection or smart filter over creatives |
| Template version | Saved campaign, ad-set, budget, and naming settings |
| Launch | Submitted work with recorded inputs and operation results |
| Schedule | Recurring launch configuration and limits |

The library belongs to the workspace. Launches target an ad account. Approval and execution require their respective permissions. Account limits cap the campaigns and configured budgets that Launcher can create; they are not a cap on all Meta account spend.

## Prepare and launch

1. [Upload or import media](/launcher/media).
2. [Save and approve a creative version](/launcher/creatives).
3. [Create a template](/launcher/templates).
4. Select creatives or a [collection](/launcher/collections).
5. [Prepare and submit a launch](/launcher/launch).
6. Inspect its [results and recovery options](/launcher/recovery).

Through MCP, call `launcher_discover` to check available tools, limits, and setup requirements.

## Preview and usage

A preview estimates selected creatives, writes, budget exposure, and usage. It reserves nothing. Submission checks current approvals, access, billing, and limits again.

Superlaunch records usage for confirmed changes in Meta. Check **Billing** for your allowance and recorded usage.
