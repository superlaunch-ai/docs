---
title: "Create launch templates"
description: "Save versioned campaign settings, budgets, destinations, and naming."
---

A template holds settings you can reuse across launches. Saving an edit creates a new template version; earlier launches keep the settings they used.

## Create a template

Open **Launcher → Templates** and select **Create template**. Set:

- Campaign objective and budget ownership.
- Daily or lifetime budget, currency, and amount.
- Destination, optimization goal, billing event, and promotion details.
- Targeting and delivery settings.
- Allowed creative formats and language mode.
- Grouping and object naming.

Use the available selectors for provider objects such as Pages and promotion assets. Resolve exact IDs instead of entering an object name as an ID.

## Budget and delivery

Confirm that the budget currency matches the account. The web interface accepts displayed currency amounts; MCP fields ending in `_minor` use integer minor units. For USD, `1000` means $10.00.

Review bid strategy, bid amount, or ROAS constraints where the chosen objective requires them. A ROAS value is a ratio, not a currency amount.

Set the delivery time zone before entering start and end times. A launch schedule controls when Superlaunch starts work; template delivery settings control the provider's delivery times.

## Reuse or revise

Save the template and choose the version during [launch setup](/launcher/launch). Through MCP, use `launcher_templates_get` to inspect its versions and `launcher_template_versions_create` to save new settings.

Archive a template to remove it from active use. Keep earlier version IDs when reviewing an existing launch or schedule.
