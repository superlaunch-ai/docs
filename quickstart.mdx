Connect a Meta ad account and check your first report. You need a Meta identity with access to that account. A workspace owner must connect it to Superlaunch.

## 1. Sign in and choose a workspace

Open [Superlaunch](https://app.superlaunch.pro/sign-in). Create an account or sign in, then create a workspace or accept your invitation. Complete any email verification shown during sign-in.

If you belong to more than one workspace, choose the workspace you want to use from the account menu. Connections, permissions, and billing belong to that workspace.

## 2. Connect an ad account

Open **Connections → Ad platforms**. A workspace owner can select **Connect Meta**, complete the provider authorization, and choose the accounts to connect.

Allow time for the first data import. An account can be connected before its assets and reporting data finish loading. See [Connect ad platforms](/workspace/connections) if an account is missing.

## 3. Check a report

Open **Insights**, choose an ad account, and set a reporting period. Start with spend, impressions, and clicks. Confirm the account currency and date range before interpreting results.

An empty report can mean that the first import is still running, that the account had no delivery in that period, or that you lack reporting access.

## 4. Connect an AI assistant (optional)

Follow [Connect an MCP client](/mcp/connect) for ChatGPT, Claude, or Codex. Approve read access for the workspace, then ask:

> List the ad accounts I can access in Superlaunch. Show their names, currencies, and time zones.

Your agent should call `ad_accounts_list` and return only accounts you can read.
