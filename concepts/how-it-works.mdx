---
title: "How Superlaunch works"
description: "Analyze ad performance, reuse creative work, and automate campaign tasks with your team or an AI agent."
---

Superlaunch helps performance marketers, agencies, in-house user acquisition teams, and brand owners manage Meta ads across multiple accounts. Use the app or an AI assistant to analyze performance, prepare campaigns, and automate routine decisions.

## Find what needs attention

Compare spend and results across campaigns, ad sets, and ads. Ask an agent questions such as “Which ads spent the most last week?” or “How did performance change by country?” Use the same reporting period when you compare results, and check how far the imported data extends.

Start with [Analyze performance](/guides/insights).

## Reuse your creative work

Keep images, videos, and approved copy in a shared library. Use Launcher templates to reuse campaign settings, then select the creatives for a batch or a scheduled launch. Your team can reuse the same approved content across launches.

For example, prepare several new creatives for an existing ad set, preview the planned changes, and launch them together. See [Prepare and submit a launch](/launcher/launch).

## Automate routine checks

Create rules for conditions you would otherwise check by hand. For example, set a rule to pause an ad when its spend crosses a limit and its results do not meet your target. Run a rule manually to review its outcome, or schedule it to check while you are away.

An agent can help you [prepare a rule](/guides/rules). Check a dry run before enabling live changes. Submitted runs continue after you close the conversation.

## Give your team and agents controlled access

Share [brand guidance](/guides/brands), creative work, and account access in one workspace. Choose which workspace and scopes an agent can use when you connect it. Your team’s current permissions still determine which accounts and actions it can access.

You can use an agent for analysis while keeping campaign changes limited to the people and connections you authorize.

## See what happened

Open **Activity** to review tool calls, campaign actions, and rule runs. Check whether an operation completed, which target it affected, and whether it needs attention. A submitted request may still be running, so inspect its final result before counting the work as complete.

## Start with one task

Connect an ad account, choose a reporting period, and compare your campaigns. Then use a rule for a repeated check or Launcher for your next creative batch. You can add more accounts and invite your team as your workflow grows.
