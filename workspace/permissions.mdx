---
title: "Team and permissions"
description: "Manage workspace members and control access to accounts, rules, and launches."
---

Your workspace owner manages members and their permissions from **Team**. The same permissions apply in the web app and through MCP.

## Invite a teammate

1. Open **Team** in the correct workspace.
2. Enter the email address the teammate will use to sign in.
3. Choose the workspace role and ad-account roles you may assign.
4. Send the invitation and check its queued, sent, or failed status.
5. Ask the teammate to accept the email link and verify their email if required.

You can resend or revoke a pending invitation. On acceptance, Superlaunch applies the saved roles after it checks current access.

Membership and account access are separate. A teammate can belong to your workspace without permission to read a particular ad account.

## Review permissions

Open the member's permissions. Grant the required functions for the relevant ad accounts, such as reading assets or insights, managing rules, or performing provider actions.

Custom metric reads need current account and Insights access. Imports and definition changes have separate account grants. A team manager can issue an API key for selected accounts and operations from **Team → API keys**. The key's issuer must keep their own access. See [Custom metrics API](/reference/custom-metrics-api).

Launcher uses its own read, author, execution, scheduling, and control permissions. Authoring a creative does not imply permission to launch it. Use the available permission definitions in the member editor.

Brand and memory access also depend on their scope. A private memory belongs to its author; team, brand, and ad-account memories follow their corresponding access rules.

## MCP access

An MCP connection uses the member who authorized it. `mcp:read` permits read tools; `mcp:write` permits write tools in addition to read access. These scopes do not expand the member's account or functional permissions.

Superlaunch rechecks membership, connection status, and target access on tool calls. If access changes, an existing conversation can lose access too.

## Resolve an access error

Check the workspace, account, member permissions, OAuth scopes, and connection status. If a rule targets several accounts, the member needs the required access to its complete target set.

See [MCP authentication](/mcp/authentication) and [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting).
