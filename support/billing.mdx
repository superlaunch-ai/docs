title: "Billing and usage"
description: "Review the workspace subscription, usage, payments, and access state."
---

The workspace owner manages billing. Open **Billing** from the workspace controls to review the current subscription, plan limits, usage, and payments.

## Plans and trials

Use the [pricing page](https://superlaunch.pro/pricing) and the checkout summary for current prices, billing intervals, trial terms, taxes, and included limits. Review the billing workspace before starting checkout.

A checkout return page is not proof of an active subscription. Wait for Superlaunch to show the confirmed billing state. If it remains pending, refresh the billing view or contact support before opening another checkout.

## Usage

Review **Billing** for the current allowance and recorded usage. In Launcher, previews estimate usage if provider writes succeed. Submission and polling do not themselves confirm provider-write usage.

Your plan and account limits can prevent new work. Check the billing result and error message rather than repeatedly submitting the same launch.

## Payments and subscription management

Use the subscription controls to review available plan changes, cancellation, or resumption. Read the displayed effective date before confirming a change.

The payments view shows recent payment activity where available. Payment-method management can open the provider's hosted billing portal; embedded checkout and saved-card management are separate flows.

If you see a sandbox or test-mode notice, follow its instructions. Test-mode behavior does not prove that a live charge completed.

## Restricted access

A workspace can have full, read-only, or recovery access depending on its billing state. Read-only access can permit review while preventing new writes. Recovery access exposes the supported controls for resolving interrupted work.

For a billing access message, the owner should inspect the current status in **Billing**. Other members should contact the workspace owner.

For payment help or refund questions, use [Contact](https://superlaunch.pro/contact) and the [refund policy](https://superlaunch.pro/refund.html).
