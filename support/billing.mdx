The workspace owner manages billing. Open **Billing** to review the subscription, plan limits, usage, and payments.

## Plans and trials

Check the [pricing page](https://superlaunch.pro/pricing) for current plans. Before starting a subscription, review the workspace, price, billing interval, trial terms, and taxes in checkout.

After checkout, return to **Billing** and check the subscription status. If it stays pending, refresh the page or [contact support](https://superlaunch.pro/contact) before starting another checkout.

## Usage

Review your allowance and recorded usage in **Billing**. Launcher previews estimate usage; Superlaunch records usage after it confirms changes in Meta. If a limit blocks a launch, review the message and your allowance before trying again.

## Manage your subscription and payments

Use **Manage subscription** to review plan changes or **Cancel subscription** to cancel. Check the effective date before confirming a change.

Open **Payments** for payment activity. Select **View invoices and manage billing** to open the payment provider's billing portal.

If the page shows **Test mode**, no real payments are processed.

## Restore access

A billing issue can restrict new work. The owner should check the status in **Billing** and follow the displayed recovery steps. Other members should contact the owner.

For payment help or refund questions, use [Contact](https://superlaunch.pro/contact) and the [refund policy](https://superlaunch.pro/refund.html).
