---
title: "Save and manage memory"
description: "Retain approved facts with private, team, brand, or ad-account scope."
---

Memory stores facts you want to reuse in later conversations. Save a fact when you ask your agent to remember it or approve a focused suggestion to save it.

## Choose a scope

| Scope | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Private | Your personal working preferences |
| Team | Facts relevant to the workspace |
| Brand | Guidance specific to a brand |
| Ad account | Facts specific to an advertising account |

Brand and ad-account scopes require the corresponding Superlaunch UUID. Private and team scopes do not take a target ID.

## Save a fact

> Remember for this brand that campaign copy must use British English and avoid discount claims unless I provide an approved offer.

The agent should resolve the brand and call `memory_remember` with your fact, scope, request UUID, and consent value. It should report whether the result is active, pending indexing, duplicated, rejected, or needs confirmation.

A stored fact awaiting indexing might not appear in semantic recall yet.

## Review and correct

Open **Memory** to review available records. Through MCP, `memory_list` lists records and `memory_get` reads an exact record. `memory_recall` finds relevant context for a query.

Read the current version before using `memory_update`, `memory_set_enabled`, or `memory_forget`. Disable a record to stop future retrieval; forget it when you want permanent deletion.

## Interpret recalled facts

Recalled memory contains user-authored context. The agent should use it as context and evaluate it against your current request. A stored fact can be incomplete or outdated; it should not override your current instruction or grant new permission.
